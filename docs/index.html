What Safety AI does

Six layers of protection.
One quiet extension.

Safety AI runs in the background on the AI sites your child uses. No accounts, no dashboards to babysit, no data leaving your machine.

01

Safe Mode

Real-time content scanning on allowed AI sites flags harmful or inappropriate keywords the moment they appear.

02

Screen-time limits

Set a daily cap in minutes. Access pauses automatically once the limit is reached — no negotiation, no workarounds.

03

Usage insight

See exactly how much time is spent across AI tools with 1D, 7D, 30D and 1Y charts — all stored locally.

04

Allow / block list

Pre-loaded for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot, Perplexity and more. Add your own custom sites.

05

Parent password

PBKDF2-hashed parent password protects every setting. A 60-second inactivity auto-lock keeps unlocked sessions safe.

06

On-screen reminders

A discreet, draggable timer widget shows remaining time and gentle safety prompts while the child browses.

Inside the extension

Calm, quiet, unmistakable.

How it works

Three minutes to set up.
Then it just runs.

Step 01

Load the extension

Open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, then click Load unpacked and select the SafekidExtension folder.

Step 02

Set a parent password

Choose a password on first run. It's hashed with PBKDF2 and stored locally. Only you can change settings after that.

Step 03

Pick sites & limits

Enable Safe Mode, set a daily cap in minutes, and choose which AI sites are allowed or blocked. That's it.

Privacy

Nothing leaves the device.

Most parental tools trade privacy for control — accounts, cloud dashboards, telemetry, a growing profile of your child. Safety AI takes the opposite path. All settings and history live in chrome.storage.local on your own machine.

No accounts, ever.

No sign-in, no email, no identifier tied to your child.

No analytics, no telemetry.

The extension never phones home. There is no home.

No servers.

The whole thing is local code and static rules — audit it on GitHub.
